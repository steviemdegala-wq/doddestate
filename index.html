About the Estate

Where love stories begin.

Nestled on a stunning 135+-acre ranch in Gilmer, Texas, The Dodd Estate is an iconic white home and meticulously maintained grounds — built by George and Claudia Dodd and treasured by the Enoch community for years.

Today, the family continues their tradition of hosting weddings, celebrations, and milestone gatherings in the heart of East Texas.
